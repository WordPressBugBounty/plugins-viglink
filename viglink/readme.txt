== Description ==
Sovrn Commerce helps you maximize your affiliate revenue. Turn every link into a revenue stream with link optimization, price comparisons, AI shopping galleries, and unified reporting.

By [signing up for Sovrn](https://platform.sovrn.com/account/signup) and installing this plugin, you can automatically monetize links to over 50,000 merchants. [Sovrn Commerce](https://www.sovrn.com/commerce/) works on existing links across all of your pages through link optimization. It even works for links distributed in your RSS feeds.

After you’ve signed up, sign in to [sovrn.com](https://platform.sovrn.com/account/signin) to view real-time analytics and full-funnel reporting, understand buyer expectations, and optimize earnings across your content.

When clicks leave your site, you are creating value. Sovrn Commerce is the easiest way to measure and capture that value.

== Installation ==

1. Upload the `viglink` folder to `/wp-content/plugins/`
1. Activate the plugin through the 'Plugins' menu in WordPress
1. If you don't already have a Sovrn account, [sign up](https://platform.sovrn.com/account/signup)
1. Sign in to [Sovrn Platform](https://platform.sovrn.com/account) and copy your API key from your account
1. Insert the key from above in the Sovrn plugin settings on your site
1. Configure the plugin in the WordPress Settings > Sovrn menu
